is not a four-arm verdict. The machine-readable artefacts are in
`reports/20261106/v2/`; this is the human reading of them.

## Statistics

- Primary family {C−A, B−A} (D−C not run): C−A two-sided p=0.278, B−A p=0.486;
  Holm p=0.556 for both. Neither comparison is significant.
- Arm vs benchmark (descriptive, not Holm-adjusted): all six CIs exclude zero
  with negative differences. A−BENCH_20K p2=0.022; C−BENCH_20K p2=0.040;
  B−BENCH_25K p2=0.014. The consistent direction is the evidence.
- DSR: A 0.673, B 0.592, C 0.637 (below the conventional 0.95 bar).
- PBO (CSCV, 10 splits, A/B/C): 0.881. Selecting the best arm is mostly
  selecting noise.
- 2× cost stress: A +7.20%, B +8.28%, C +10.72%; benchmarks +24.83% / +31.34%;
  C−A Holm p=0.48, B−A 0.59. The conclusion does not depend on the cost assumption.
- 10-seed sweep: A/B/C and the reversed control are identical across seeds
  (0.00% stdev); the random control spans 0.02%..2.95% (mean 1.40%); the
  shuffled-history control spans 17.53%..18.36% (mean 17.86%), above every arm.

## What this says

No arm beats a simple buy-and-hold with the same entry risk budget, in absolute
return or in return per unit of drawdown. A rule whose return is reachable by
trading on a destroyed price history is not evidence of signal. The arm ranking
(C > B > A) is not reliable: PBO is high and the pairwise differences are not
significant.

## What this does not say

- It does not test D. There is no weekly LLM engine with persisted
  request/response hashes; D−C is unevaluated and the verdict is `INSUFFICIENT`.
- C is a fixed SMA20/50 rule, not the non-LLM automatic search the G4 design
  calls for. B and C are standalone SPY rules, not core sleeve plus satellites.
  The "value of automatic optimization" and "core + rule" questions are untested.
- The universe is one symbol (SPY). Cross-asset risk budgeting, VIX, FRED,
  EDGAR and news are not in this dataset.
- No parameter is fitted, so purging and embargo have nothing to separate; they
  become required the moment a search arm is added.
- `min_effect` is 0.0 because the registered spec declared no economic floor.
- It is historical development evidence, not unseen data. The only unseen test
  is forward data, which needs a candidate that does not yet exist.

## Next

1. Do not promote any arm to G5 on this evidence; G5 stays `NOT_RUN`.
2. Fix the experiment design before another arm is added: same position-sizing
   rule across arms, core-plus-satellite composition, a multi-symbol universe,
   and — if the search question is to be answered — a real walk-forward search
   arm with purging and embargo.
3. Decide D: build the weekly LLM engine (20–55 hours of compute per replay),
   or pre-register a three-arm confirmatory experiment with a declared
   `min_effect` and a forward window.
4. Keep both benchmarks, the negative controls and the seed sweep in every
   future report.
