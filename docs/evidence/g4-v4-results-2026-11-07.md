## Correction to v3 (research-only)

The v3 bars were **raw**. Splits are price jumps in a raw series: XLE and XLB −50.2% on 2025-12-05 (2-for-1), NVDA −89.9% on 2024-06-10, AAPL −74.1% on 2020-08-31. Every v3 row that holds XLE booked a loss that never happened (all arms, benchmarks and controls). v3's numbers are therefore **not reliable** and its note carries a correction. v3 also traded TLT (hold-only in production), IWM and GLD (not on the allowlist), so it was never a production-permitted experiment.

For information only, the v3 design was re-run on adjusted bars (`reports/20261107/v3b-adjusted/`, **post hoc, not registered**): B +43.6% against +31.1% on raw bars; B − A Holm 0.12, C − B 0.20; B and C still below exposure-matched holding. The qualitative conclusion held, the numbers moved by up to 12 points.

## What this does not say

- Sub-books do not share cash; account limits are checked on the sum afterwards.
- Single-stock satellites (NVDA, MSFT, AAPL) have one 2020–2026 path each; nothing here generalizes to stocks.
- Dividends are in the adjusted prices (total return), but fills happen at adjusted prices.
- Historical development evidence. 2020–2026 has been looked at many times; the unseen test is forward data.
