## What this says

On this universe and window, neither a fixed trend rule nor a searched one adds anything over the core sleeve that survives statistics. Both lose to simply holding the same average exposure, and both lose to random timing at that exposure. The core sleeve itself has a return/DD close to its exposure-matched hold (3.98 vs 3.85): it is a risk-sizing product, not an edge. No candidate goes to G5.
