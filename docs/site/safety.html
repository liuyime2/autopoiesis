What the Guardian refuses to let a configuration do

Every limit is checked at the point it enters, because a limit that cannot be expressed is not a limit. NaN <= 0 is False, so a NaN position cap sailed through a positivity test and reached the Guardian, where every comparison against it is also False - a 10,000-unit buy that a 5,000 limit correctly refuses was approved. Nothing reported it, because the refusals simply stopped happening.

What is not claimed

  • Paper results are not live results. No broker fees were observed in this paper record. The reported net charges an explicitly assumed round-trip cost on the notional actually traded, and the page says so.
  • No figure here is a certification. The target remains unproven, and the record holds fewer trading days than the target window.
