A research instrument, not a product. It trades a paper account, grades its own strategies against broker-confirmed fills, retires the ones that do not earn their place, and publishes every claim it makes with the command that produced it.

What it has shown

  • The accounting is real: broker-confirmed fills, a FIFO lot ledger, and a Guardian whose limits cannot be weakened by any configuration value.
  • The gate can fail, and is made to fail on purpose to prove it. make verify-self-test plants the defects each class exists to catch, including a NaN limit that silently stops comparing.
  • Research requires a registered contract and validated real bars before evaluating candidates and recording trials. Scheduled operation requires a configured contract, usable cache and verified successful runs.

What it has not shown

  • That it beats holding. UNKNOWN after cost against SPY UNKNOWN over 20 of 60 recorded dates. Allocated capital and aligned equity are unavailable.
  • Any directional edge. 129 hypotheses appear in the historical signal ledger. These aggregate verdict counts do not establish validated alpha or certify statistical error control.
  • Live trading. Not present. live mode is refused by the configuration loader.
