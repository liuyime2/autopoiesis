## What the evidence supports, and does not

The sleeve halves the worst drawdown (-28.9% vs -55.2%) and lifts Sharpe (0.75 vs 0.65) over 33
years of SPY, and gives up return in every period (2023-: CAGR 12.6% vs 22.2%, Sharpe 1.23 vs 1.42).
It is a risk product. No tested price-direction signal survived (173 signal tests, 0 directional
survivors), so nothing here is an edge, and "makes money" is only certifiable by the 60-valid-day
paper benchmark (27/60 on 10-23).
